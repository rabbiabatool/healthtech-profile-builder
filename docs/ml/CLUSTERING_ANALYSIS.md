# Unsupervised Learning Analysis: Engagement Clustering & PCA

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Educational & Operational Use Only**: EXPLORATORY OPERATIONAL ARCHETYPES: These clusters are generated by unsupervised algorithms on non-sensitive operational scheduling and engagement patterns. They are exploratory groupings intended for clinic resource planning, scheduling logistics, and reminder cadence optimization. They do NOT represent clinical diagnoses, health risk categories, medical status, or fixed patient personality traits. K-Means enforces spherical boundaries across a continuous spectrum of engagement and should never be used to restrict or deprioritize healthcare access.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In accordance with Week 4 objectives, this unsupervised learning module explores behavioral clustering over **non-sensitive operational engagement features** in the synthetic clinic appointment dataset. The goal is to identify distinct operational archetypes to assist scheduling logistics, reminder cadence design, and clinic resource allocation—**without relying on clinical diagnoses or demographic attributes**.

---

## 2. Feature Selection & Preprocessing

- **Dataset Size**: 20000 records (5765 synthetic patients).
- **Non-Sensitive Features Evaluated**:
  1. `lead_time_days`: Days between scheduling and appointment date.
  2. `scheduled_hour`: Hour of day of the appointment.
  3. `previous_appointments`: Total historical visits.
  4. `previous_no_shows`: Prior unfulfilled appointments.
  5. `attendance_ratio`: Derived engagement reliability $\frac{\text{attended}}{\text{total} + 1}$.
  6. `distance_km`: Travel distance (median imputed for null values).
  7. `reminder_sent`: Reminder receipt indicator (0 or 1).
- **Strict Privacy Safeguard**: Zero demographic columns (`age`, `gender`, `race`, `zip_code`) or diagnostic fields were included.
- **Scaling**: All continuous attributes were scaled using `StandardScaler` to zero mean and unit variance.

## 6. Comparison of Methods: K-Means vs. Agglomerative Clustering

- **Adjusted Rand Index (ARI)**: `0.4866`
- **Normalized Mutual Information (NMI)**: `0.4776`
- **Method Differences**:
  K-Means and Agglomerative Clustering show moderate-to-high agreement (ARI = 0.4866, NMI = 0.4776). Differences stem from K-Means optimizing spherical variance around geometric centroids, whereas Agglomerative (Ward linkage) builds hierarchical merges minimizing intra-cluster variance without centroid rigidity.

## 7. Meaningful vs. Misleading Clusters Analysis

### Meaningful Utility
1. **Reminder Cadence Optimization**: Patients booking weeks in advance benefit from multi-stage reminders (14-day email + 2-day SMS), whereas short-notice bookers require instant confirmations.
2. **Clinic Staffing Logistics**: Understanding morning vs. afternoon visit concentrations allows balanced staffing without overbooking.

### Misleading Risks & Ethical Safeguards
1. **Boundary Artifacts**: K-Means enforces sharp geometric partitions across continuous behavioral gradients. A patient near a centroid boundary does not belong fundamentally to a different category than one just across the threshold.
2. **No Clinical Inference**: Engagement habits are not medical symptoms. Assuming low attendance history indicates poor health compliance or moral failing is scientifically invalid and harmful.
3. **Fluidity of Human Schedules**: Cluster assignments change as patients change jobs, move homes, or resolve transportation hurdles. They are snapshots of operational data, never permanent labels.

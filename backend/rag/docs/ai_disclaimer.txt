GREENLEAF CLINIC – EDUCATIONAL AI ASSISTANT DISCLAIMER (Version 1.0, Effective 2026-01-01)
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT – EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

1. PURPOSE OF THIS TOOL
   The Greenleaf Clinic AI FAQ Assistant is a prototype educational tool designed to help
   members find information about clinic booking, cancellation, privacy, and general
   administrative policies. It is built as a demonstration of Retrieval-Augmented Generation
   (RAG) technology using only the fictional Greenleaf Clinic policy documents.

2. WHAT THIS TOOL CAN DO
   The assistant can answer questions about:
   - How to book, cancel, or reschedule appointments.
   - What appointment types are available and how long they take.
   - Confirmation and reminder processes.
   - Data privacy rights and how the clinic uses member information.
   - Fee structures and cancellation notice requirements.

3. WHAT THIS TOOL CANNOT DO
   The assistant CANNOT and WILL NOT:
   - Provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical advice of any kind.
   - Access individual member health records, appointment history, or personal data.
   - Guarantee the accuracy of information outside the approved policy documents.
   - Replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
   - Process bookings, cancellations, or any transactional requests.

4. LIMITATIONS AND SCOPE
   All responses are generated from pre-approved fictional policy documents only. If a question
   falls outside the scope of approved documents, the assistant will explicitly state:
   "I don't have enough information in the approved clinic policies to answer that question."
   The assistant will not speculate, invent, or hallucinate information beyond its approved sources.

5. MEDICAL ADVICE DISCLAIMER
   NO RESPONSE FROM THIS TOOL CONSTITUTES MEDICAL ADVICE. If you have a health concern,
   please contact your healthcare provider or call emergency services if urgent.

6. CONTACT FOR CORRECTIONS
   If you believe an AI response contains an error or is misleading, please report it to
   feedback@greenleafclinic.example. The clinic reviews feedback weekly.

GREENLEAF CLINIC – CANCELLATION AND RESCHEDULING POLICY (Version 1.4, Effective 2026-01-01)
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT – EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

1. CANCELLATION NOTICE REQUIREMENTS
   Members are asked to cancel or reschedule appointments with as much notice as possible.
   The minimum required notice periods are:
   - SPECIALIST appointments: 48 hours before the scheduled time.
   - CHECKUP and FOLLOW_UP appointments: 24 hours before the scheduled time.
   - VACCINATION and LAB_TEST appointments: 12 hours before the scheduled time.
   Cancellations within these windows are recorded as short-notice cancellations.

2. HOW TO CANCEL
   Cancellations may be submitted via:
   (a) The Greenleaf Clinic Member Portal → My Appointments → Cancel.
   (b) Phone: call reception during business hours (Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00).
   (c) Email: appointments@greenleafclinic.example (confirmation required within 2 business hours).
   Walk-in cancellations at the front desk are also accepted.

3. NO-SHOW POLICY
   A no-show is recorded when a member does not attend a confirmed appointment and provides
   no advance cancellation notice. After three no-shows within a rolling 12-month period,
   the clinic may require a confirmation deposit for future bookings. This deposit is fully
   refunded upon attendance.

4. RESCHEDULING
   Members may reschedule an appointment at no charge provided the original slot is freed
   within the required notice window. Rescheduling requests may be made through the same
   channels as cancellations. Members may reschedule the same appointment a maximum of
   two times; a third rescheduling request will require a call to reception for manual review.

5. CLINIC-INITIATED CANCELLATIONS
   In rare circumstances (staff illness, equipment failure, emergency facility closure), the
   clinic may cancel or reschedule an appointment. Members will be contacted at least 2 hours
   before the appointment and offered the earliest available alternative slot. No cancellation
   fee applies in these situations.

6. FEE IMPLICATIONS
   Short-notice cancellations or no-shows for SPECIALIST appointments may incur a service fee
   per the current fee schedule. Members on hardship exemption plans are not subject to
   cancellation fees. Fee waivers for exceptional circumstances must be requested in writing
   within 7 days of the missed appointment.

GREENLEAF CLINIC – APPOINTMENT BOOKING POLICY (Version 2.1, Effective 2026-01-01)
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT – EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

1. BOOKING CHANNELS
   Appointments may be booked online through the Greenleaf Clinic Member Portal, by calling the
   clinic reception on business days (Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00), or in person at the front desk.
   Walk-in appointments are accepted subject to availability and are not guaranteed.

2. ADVANCE BOOKING WINDOW
   Appointments may be booked up to 180 days in advance. Same-day bookings are available for
   urgent non-emergency needs if slots remain. A minimum of 2 hours notice is required for
   same-day bookings made by phone or online.

3. APPOINTMENT TYPES
   The clinic offers the following appointment categories:
   - CHECKUP: Routine general health screening (30 minutes).
   - FOLLOW_UP: Continuity visit following a previous consultation (20 minutes).
   - VACCINATION: Immunisation administration (15 minutes).
   - LAB_TEST: Blood draw or specimen collection (10–20 minutes, fasting may be required).
   - SPECIALIST: Referral-required specialist consultation (45 minutes).

4. CONFIRMATION AND REMINDERS
   A booking confirmation is sent by email within 15 minutes of scheduling. SMS reminders are
   sent to members who have opted in: once at 48 hours before the appointment, and once again
   2 hours before. Members may opt out of reminders at any time through the Member Portal
   under Settings → Notifications.

5. IDENTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
   Members must present their Greenleaf Clinic membership card or government-issued photo ID
   at check-in. Failure to present valid ID may result in the appointment being rescheduled.

6. LATE ARRIVAL POLICY
   Members arriving more than 10 minutes late for a scheduled appointment may be asked to
   reschedule. The clinic will make reasonable efforts to accommodate late arrivals if clinical
   capacity permits, but this cannot be guaranteed.

7. APPOINTMENT FEES AND BILLING
   Standard membership covers CHECKUP and FOLLOW_UP appointments at no additional charge.
   VACCINATION, LAB_TEST, and SPECIALIST appointments may incur additional fees depending on
   the member's plan tier. A full schedule of fees is available at reception and on the Member
   Portal under Billing.

8. MINORS AND DEPENDANTS
   Members may book appointments for registered dependants under 18 years of age. A parent or
   legal guardian must accompany dependants under 16 at all appointments. Dependant profiles
   must be added to the member account before booking.

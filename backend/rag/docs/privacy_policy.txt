GREENLEAF CLINIC – MEMBER PRIVACY AND DATA POLICY (Version 3.0, Effective 2026-01-01)
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT – EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

1. DATA WE COLLECT
   Greenleaf Clinic collects the following information from members:
   - Registration data: name, date of birth, contact email, phone number, and address.
   - Appointment records: dates, types, attendance history, and scheduling metadata.
   - Optional engagement data: reminder preferences and communication channel opt-ins.
   The clinic does not collect race, ethnicity, religion, disability status, or financial
   account details as part of the member profile.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
   Member data is used solely for:
   (a) Scheduling and managing appointments.
   (b) Sending appointment confirmations and reminders (if opted in).
   (c) Internal operational analytics to improve clinic scheduling efficiency.
   (d) Regulatory compliance and audit record-keeping.
   Member data is NEVER sold, rented, or shared with third-party marketing organisations.

3. DATA RETENTION
   Appointment and engagement records are retained for 7 years in line with health
   administration regulations. After the retention period, records are securely deleted.
   Members may request deletion of non-mandatory data (e.g. reminder preferences) at any time.

4. MEMBER DATA RIGHTS
   Members have the right to:
   - Access a copy of their stored data (request via the Member Portal or in writing).
   - Correct inaccurate data within 14 business days of a written request.
   - Withdraw consent for optional data processing (reminders, communications) at any time.
   - Lodge a complaint with the relevant Data Protection Authority.

5. SECURITY MEASURES
   All member data is stored encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is restricted to
   clinic staff with a documented operational need. The clinic undergoes annual security
   audits and maintains ISO-27001-equivalent controls.

6. EDUCATIONAL AI DISCLAIMER
   The Member Portal includes an AI-assisted FAQ tool for educational purposes. This tool
   answers questions based only on approved clinic policy documents. It does NOT have access
   to individual member health records, diagnosis data, or personal information. All AI
   responses are generated from fictional policy text and must not be treated as medical,
   legal, or financial advice.

7. CONTACT
   Data queries may be directed to privacy@greenleafclinic.example.
